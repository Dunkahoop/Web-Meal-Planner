IMPORTANT!

There are some units that I can't convert to grams (i.e. a pinch of salt, a dash of pepper). Please use your best judgement when adding these to the grocery list. For something like "One diced onion", I would recommend adding the ingredient as an individual unit Also, the conversion of spoons and cups to grams is approximate, as many ingrtedients have different densities and accounting for all of them would be a lot of work and may make things more confusing.
